## Original repository graphics

`assets/evopolis-cover.png` is AI-generated conceptual artwork created with the built-in image-generation tool and restyled as an original 16-bit pixel-art town at the user's request. The exact generation/edit prompt is preserved in `assets/cover-prompt.txt`. It is not a screenshot of working software, a representation of actual participants, or an empirical result.

`assets/research-loop.svg` and `assets/commons-cycle.svg` are editable vector diagrams of the proposed research architecture and resource mechanism. Their arrows represent dependencies and resource flows, not measured effects.

All current graphics follow the NES/SNES-inspired direction in [VISUAL_STYLE.md](VISUAL_STYLE.md). The matching CSS in `assets/evopolis-theme.css` is a reusable starting point for the future local interface. The empirical figure's retro styling preserves its recorded points, aggregation, and uncertainty intervals.

## Working community viewer

`evopolis/static/town.js` draws original pixel residents and town scenery directly on Canvas. It uses no copied game sprites or external runtime assets. A–D mark recorded group positions and do not attribute demographics or private beliefs to participants. Resource flowers and motion are schematic presentation, not empirical magnitudes or added environmental rules.

`assets/community-viewer.png` is a real Chromium screenshot of the working local app at 1366×900: human Experiment 1, Equal allocation, launch `67430636`, episode `0`, playback round 1. Its underlying records have the same pinned CSV provenance and CC BY 4.0 attribution as the Task 01 figure. The [viewer verification](../results/task02/verification.json) records the screenshot checksum, cache provenance and identified example. No generated artwork is presented as a software screenshot.

## New behavioral fits

Task 03 uses only the four explicitly labeled human Experiment 1 conditions in that pinned CSV. The nine normalized pre-decision inputs follow the paper's Methods, “Training virtual players (BC1).” The four model families, inflated beta-binomial emission, training implementation and released checkpoints are new EvoPolis work under the [fixed Task 03 specification](tasks/03-trained-behavioral-agents.md); they are not copied upstream networks or weights. The [split manifest](../results/task03/split.json) and [experiment report](behavioral-agents.md) preserve the human-data provenance and CC BY 4.0 attribution.

Task 03 learning and prediction figures come from measured training logs and held-out human predictions. The `trained-outcomes` and `trained-trajectories` figures depict newly sampled model behavior, with held-out human groups identified separately. All plot sources produce editable SVG and PNG outputs in the established palette without altering data geometry. The trained-town screenshot is captured from the running application; it does not establish human behavioral fidelity.

## Conditional collective forecasts

Task 04 continues the original six neural fits and forecasts from observed prefixes of the same Experiment 1 test groups. Human records retain the pinned Koster, Pîslar et al./DeepMind data provenance and CC BY 4.0 attribution. The original model, training, generation and numerical-environment sources remain byte-identical; the continuation, conditional-forecast and scoring modules are new EvoPolis implementations of the [Task 04 declaration](tasks/04-collective-forecast-fidelity.md).

`assets/task04-optimization`, `task04-forecast-scores`, `task04-renewal-calibration` and `task04-forecast-trajectories` are measured PNG/SVG figures produced by [forecast_plot.py](../evopolis/forecast_plot.py). They distinguish actual human futures, newly sampled branches, forecast-distribution bands, training-seed variation and group-bootstrap intervals. `assets/forecast-community-viewer.png` is a real screenshot of the local forecast view, not generated artwork. [The forecast report](collective-forecast-fidelity.md) and saved configuration, seeds, checkpoint hashes and archive provenance identify the evidence. No Experiment 2–3 outcomes were added to this study.
